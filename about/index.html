About Pun Labs

Pun Labs is a novelty gift, office and art supply company. It makes Pop Colors, a line of colored pencil sets that turn a favorite show, film, band or painter into a box of 12 punny pencils.

The business was already established when its current owner took it on, with Pop Colors sold through its own store, Amazon, Etsy and independent retailers. Today Pun Labs works out of a studio in Charlotte, North Carolina, where it designs new sets and is developing Serious Supply, its first office line.

Pun Labs is part of Universal Benefit Corporation.
