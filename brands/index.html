Pop Colors

Pop Colors celebrates pop culture and nostalgia, one pencil at a time.

Each set holds 12 pre-sharpened hexagonal colored pencils, and each pencil is foil-stamped with a pun or reference tied to its color. Sets are themed around favorite TV shows, films, music, video games and painters. Many come with coloring pages printed on heavy cardstock, sold on their own or bundled with the pencils.
